How Back2You Works

Three simple steps to get your belongings back.

📝

1. Report

Lost something or found an item? Fill a short form with details and a photo.

🔍

2. Search

Browse or filter by category, location, and date to find matching items.

🤝

3. Reconnect

Submit a claim. Once verified, the item is marked Returned and finds its way home.
